We are pleased to announce that on February 19, MESL will host the STEM Challenge 2.0 at the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Mymensingh Engineering College. This distinguished event is designed to foster innovation, problem-solving, and hands-on experience in the fields of engineering and technology.

The STEM Challenge 2.0 offers participants a unique opportunity to engage in competition, collaboration, and skill demonstration. This event will challenge participants to address real-world engineering and embedded system problems through teamwork and technical expertise.
